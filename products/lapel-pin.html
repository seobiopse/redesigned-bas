How Ordering Works

  1. Request a quote with your artwork, size and quantity.
  2. We send a free digital proof and your confirmed price.
  3. You approve the proof. Nothing goes into production without your sign-off.
  4. Your pins are made in Prospect, South Australia and shipped Australia-wide.

Choose the pin style that suits your design and budget.

Hard Enamel

Die-struck, filled with enamel colour, then polished flat for a smooth, glossy finish.

Soft Enamel

Die-struck with recessed enamel colour and raised metal lines for a textured finish.

Die Struck

Stamped metal with a classic polished or antique plated finish.

Printed

Full colour artwork printed onto metal for gradients and fine detail.
