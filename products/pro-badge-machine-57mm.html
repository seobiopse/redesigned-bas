Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our domed badges, fasteners, or artwork submission?

Does the resin dome turn yellow over time?

No, we use only high-grade UV-stable polyurethane that remains crystal clear and yellow-resistant for years, even in harsh Australian sunlight conditions. Unlike cheap epoxy alternatives, polyurethane will not become brittle or discolour.

Is the dome flexible or hard?

The polyurethane dome is semi-rigid with self-healing properties. Minor surface scuffs bounce back, keeping your brand looking flawless.

Can we order just 1 badge now and re-order identical badges later?

Yes! We archive all customer artwork files and exact badge layouts under your customer record. You can order single staff replacements whenever required with no setup fees.

How do magnetic fasteners work and are they safe for clothing?

Magnetic fasteners use two rare-earth neodymium magnets embedded in a plastic backplate. They sandwich your clothing firmly without puncturing or tearing fine silks, blazers, or cotton shirts. (Note: Not recommended for individuals with cardiac pacemakers).
