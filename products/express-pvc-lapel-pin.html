Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our express pvc lapel pin or artwork submission?

What is the difference between single-sided and double-sided printing?

Express PVC pins are produced single-sided, since the design is applied to one face of the metal base.

What types of attachments/accessories are available?

The standard fitting is a butterfly clutch, which holds the pin securely against fabric.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

The minimum order quantity for the Express PVC Lapel Pin is 25 units.

How long does production and delivery take?

Express PVC Lapel Pins take 33 working days to produce, 3 days faster than our standard lapel pin range. Delivery is quoted with your order.

Do I receive a proof before production?

Yes. Every order includes a free digital proof, and nothing goes to production until you've approved the design in writing.

What file formats do you accept for artwork? We accept JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, and EPS files.

We accept JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, and EPS files.

What's the difference between the Express PVC Lapel Pin and the standard Express Lapel Pin?

Both share the same faster 33-day turnaround and affordable pricing. Express PVC adds full-colour printing and sparkle-finish options, offering a bit more design variety than the standard Express Lapel Pin's traditional enamel finish.

What's the difference between Express PVC and Full Colour Printed lapel pins?

Both offer full colour printing sealed with a polished finish. Express PVC prioritises speed, with production 3 days faster than standard. Our Full Colour Printed Lapel Pin runs on standard 36-day production but adds a protective epoxy dome for extra scratch and UV resistance.
