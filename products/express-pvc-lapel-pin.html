What is an Express PVC Lapel Pin?

The Express PVC Lapel Pin at BadgeStore is a custom lapel pin made to order in Australia and quoted per job. What is the difference between single-sided and double-sided printing?

The difference between single-sided and double-sided printing on the Express PVC Lapel Pin at BadgeStore is that Express PVC pins are produced single-sided, since the design is applied to one face of the metal base.
