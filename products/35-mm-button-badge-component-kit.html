What else do I need to make badges with this 35mm kit?

Just two things: your own 35mm circular paper design, and a badge press like a BadgeStore machine to crimp the components together. The kit covers all the shells, covers, and pin backs.

What is the advantage of 35mm badges over the smaller 25mm size?

For the 35 mm Button Badge Component Kit at BadgeStore, more room for your design. Better visibility for detailed logos or artwork, which makes it a solid choice for promotional items or merchandise where you want the design to really stand out.

Are the components in this kit reusable?

For the 35 mm Button Badge Component Kit at BadgeStore, not once assembled. The metal shell, plastic cover, and collet crimp together permanently around your paper insert. The pin back itself, though, can open and close as many times as you like to attach the finished badge to clothing.

Can I use magnetic backs instead of the included pins?

For the 35 mm Button Badge Component Kit at BadgeStore, yes, just leave out the pin back and attach a magnetic back, butterfly clutch, or other fastener (sold separately) to the assembled badge shell instead.
