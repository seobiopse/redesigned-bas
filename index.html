Calculate Bulk Corporate Discounts How the discount is calculated
  • Choose a product, then set the quantity. The discount is a percentage off that product's list price, based on the quantity ordered.
  • Tiers: 1–9 = standard price · 10–24 = 10% off · 25–49 = 20% off · 50+ = custom B2B rates (quote).
  • The percentage applies to all units of that product.
  • Estimated Total = quantity × list price × (1 − discount).
  • Total Savings = (quantity × list price) − Estimated Total.
  • Example: 25 × 35mm Button Badge at $7.99 → 20% off → $159.80; savings $39.95.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about formats, attachments, or ordering? Look here first.

Do you support Purchase Orders for government, councils, and schools?
Yes. We support corporate invoicing and Purchase Orders (PO) for Australian educational institutions, government departments, and registered local councils. During checkout, select bank transfer invoice or send your official PO file to admin@badgestore.com.au. Payment can be settled via bank transfer within standard terms.
How do magnetic name tag attachments work? Are they safe for all garments?
Our damage-free magnetic backing uses strong neodymium double magnets mounted on a plastic backplate. By slotting the plate behind your shirt sleeve or lapel, the badge clamps securely without inserting pins. This holds firmly through suit jackets and premium fabrics without puncturing thread fibers. Note: Neodymium magnets should not be worn by employees with pacemakers.
Are safety breakaway clips available on your ID lanyards?
Yes. All of our custom corporate ID lanyards are equipped with plastic breakaway clips at the neck. In high-risk clinical, industrial, or retail settings, this safety feature ensures that if the lanyard is caught, snagged, or pulled, it snaps open instantly to protect the wearer from choking or injury.
What is the difference between soft enamel and hard enamel custom lapel pins?
Soft enamel lapel pins are baked after applying liquid color fills, leaving recessed colored wells below the raised metal borders (creating a textured 3D feel). Hard enamel pins, conversely, have paint layers overfilled and polished down completely flat with the metal frame, creating a glassy, flat, premium finish that resists scratches.
