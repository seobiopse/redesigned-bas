1. Free Digital Proofs & Written Approval

Pre-production verification process & artwork sign-off

Every custom badge, plaque, or desk sign order includes a complimentary free digital PDF proof prepared by our production artists. Production commences strictly after written approval is returned via email or our online ordering approval portal.

Once approved, custom artwork cannot be altered without additional setup and machine tooling charges. Customers are fully responsible for checking typographical details, staff titles, badge dimensions, fastner selections, and pantone/brand colour accuracy.

Artwork Precision Standards (100% Scale)

All digital proofs are rendered to 1:1 metric scale. If colour fidelity is critical, please specify exact CMYK values or Pantone Matching System (PMS) coated codes during artwork upload.

3. 30-Day Merchant Return & Guarantee

Defect protection, manufacturing warranty and refunds

BadgeStore Australia stands behind our manufacturing precision. If any item arrives defective, exhibits print degradation, magnetic clasp failure, or does not match your written approved digital proof, BadgeStore Australia will replace it free of charge or issue a full refund within 30 days of delivery.

Due to the custom-manufactured nature of personalized goods (engraved staff names, printed corporate logos), returns cannot be accepted for change of mind or customer-submitted spelling mistakes that were approved in the sign-off proof.

Manufacturing turnarounds, Australia Post tracking

Standard production orders are manufactured and dispatched within 2 to 4 business days following formal proof sign-off. Bulk orders (>500 units) or complex cast lapel pins may require extended production windows as communicated by your account representative.

5. Custom Artwork & Intellectual Property

Copyright indemnification & brand privacy protection

The client warrants that they hold the legal authority, copyright, or trademark permissions for any corporate logos, brand crests, or artwork submitted for badge manufacturing. BadgeStore Australia acts solely as a manufacturer under instructions.

Confidentiality Guarantee: We keep all proprietary vector files, employee names, and corporate registries strictly protected. We never sell, transfer, or distribute your brand assets to third-party marketing entities.

06

6. Cancellation & Order Modifications

Order alterations prior to laser or printing runs

Orders may be cancelled or altered without penalty prior to the approval of digital proofs. If an order is cancelled after proofs are rendered by our graphic studio but before production starts, a modest administrative prep fee of $25.00 + GST applies. Once printing, dome resin application, or laser etching has commenced, orders cannot be cancelled or altered.

Commercial Procurement FAQs

Can we add new staff badges later to match an existing order?

Yes! We maintain secure production master profiles for all business clients. When you onboard new employees, you can order single badges that seamlessly match the font, sizing, dome, and metal finish of your initial bulk order.

Do your prices include Australian Goods and Services Tax (GST)?

All item catalog prices display both ex-GST and inc-GST components. Full Australian compliant Tax Invoices showing our ABN (24 662 539 406) are issued automatically upon purchase completion.

What fasteners are included under the warranty?

Our heavy-duty magnetic fasteners, brooches, swivel bulldog clips, and military clutches are covered by our 30-day merchant replacement guarantee against mechanical detachment or adhesive failure.
