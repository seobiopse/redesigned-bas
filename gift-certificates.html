Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a partial balance across multiple orders?

Yes. If your total order is less than your voucher balance, the remaining amount remains tied to your certificate code for future orders of name badges or accessories.

Do BadgeStore gift certificates have an expiry date?

No. BadgeStore gift certificates never expire. In compliance with Australian Consumer Law and our service standard, vouchers are honored permanently.

Can multiple gift certificates be combined at checkout?

Yes. You can enter multiple valid voucher codes in the checkout payment screen, and any remaining invoice balance can be paid via credit card or PayPal.
