Button Badge Machines Buyer's Guide & Specifications

Materials & Craftsmanship

At BadgeStore Australia, our button badge machines include the Australian made 1800B Badge-A-Matic and three Multi Press starter kits in 25mm, 35mm and 57mm.

  • Multi Press starter kits: each includes a die set, 100 free components and a circle cutter, and accepts other die sets sold separately.
  • 1800B Badge-A-Matic: a fixed 57mm sand cast press, 6kg, supplied with 100 components and a circle cutter.
