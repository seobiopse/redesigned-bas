Backing Fasteners & Garment Safety

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding ordering, minimum quantities, artwork proofing, and attachments.

Are the machine die cutters interchangeable?

Yes, our BadgeStore professional line allows you to purchase different size dies (25mm, 32mm, 57mm) and swap them onto the same press frame using simple alignment pins.

What components are included in the starter kits?

Starter kits include the heavy-duty metal badge press, a circular die, a rotary circle paper cutter, and 100 sets of blank metal badge components (front shell, plastic mylar film, pin backplate).

Is it easy to press badges? Do we need electricity?

No electricity is needed! Our button maker uses a manual lever mechanical press. With standard printed paper and a simple two-stroke press, anyone can easily make badges.
