Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding ordering, minimum quantities, artwork proofing, and attachments.

How do I design and order custom name badges online?

Design your name badges online by picking a shape and finish — full colour print, laser engraving, acrylic dome, metal, or hardwood — entering the name and text you need, and submitting your artwork. BadgeStore manufactures every name badge to order at its Prospect, South Australia workshop with no minimum order quantity.

What materials can I choose for a custom name badge?

BadgeStore's name badge range spans full colour printed plastic, laser engraved dual-layer plastic, acrylic domed, premium metal, and laser engraved hardwood, all made to order.

Is there a minimum order quantity for name badges?

There is no minimum order quantity for name badges at BadgeStore: BadgeStore has no minimum order quantity on any of its five name badge styles. You can order a single replacement name badge at standard prices whenever a new team member joins.

How long does it take to receive custom name badges in Australia?

Receiving custom name badges in Australia from BadgeStore typically takes 1-2 business days to Adelaide metro, 2-3 business days express to Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane, and 3-5 business days express to regional areas and WA/NT, for name badges manufactured at our Prospect, South Australia workshop.

What attachment options are available for name badges?

The attachment options available for name badges at BadgeStore are, for most badges in this range, a dual neodymium magnetic backplate that holds firmly through suits and lab coats without puncturing fabric. Where magnets aren't suitable, such as near pacemakers, BadgeStore offers safety pin backs and crocodile clips instead.

What's the difference between laser-engraved and full colour name badges?

The difference between laser-engraved and full colour name badges at BadgeStore is the finish: laser-engraved name badges cut through a 2-ply plastic to reveal a contrasting letter colour underneath, giving a matte two-tone look, while full colour name badges are UV-printed directly onto acrylic for vibrant multi-colour logos. Both name badge styles are waterproof and ship from our Prospect, SA workshop.
