How do magnetic name badges work?

Magnetic name badges at BadgeStore use dual neodymium discs that clamp through fabric without puncturing it, and an extra strong magnet is available for thick jackets.

What is the difference between engraved and full colour name badges?

The difference between engraved and full colour name badges at BadgeStore is the finish: engraved badges are cut into dual-layer plastic or timber and cannot scratch off, while full colour badges are digitally printed so they can carry logos, photos and coloured backgrounds.

Can each badge have a different name?

Yes. Each name badge at BadgeStore can carry a different name: attach a name list when you order, or tell us the names in the design instructions.
