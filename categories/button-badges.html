Backing Fasteners & Garment Safety

Button badges are available with a standard pin, a garment-safe magnet or a keychain. Neodymium magnetic backings clamp securely to shirts and suit jackets without leaving pinholes or tearing fibres.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding ordering, minimum quantities, artwork proofing, and attachments.

How do I design and order custom button badges online?

Designing and ordering custom button badges online at BadgeStore takes four steps: choose a size from 25mm to 85mm, pick a pin, magnet or keychain attachment, upload your artwork (AI, SVG, PDF or PNG) or email it later, then approve the free proof. BadgeStore makes every order at its Prospect, South Australia workshop.

What sizes are available for custom button badges and fridge magnets?

The sizes available for custom button badges at BadgeStore are 25mm, 35mm, 57mm and 85mm, and custom fridge magnets are available in 35mm, 57mm and 85mm. All are printed in full colour.

Is there a minimum order quantity for button badges?

There is no minimum order quantity for button badges at BadgeStore: you can order a single badge, with the 25mm size at $6.99 for one and the unit price dropping from 10 units.

Are there bulk discounts on button badges and fridge magnets?

Bulk discounts on button badges and fridge magnets at BadgeStore start from 10 units, and the unit price keeps falling up to 10,000+ units. For example, the 35mm button badge drops from $7.99 for one badge to $3.33 at 10+, $2.15 at 25+, $1.39 at 50+ and $0.45 at 10,000+.

How do I choose between magnetic and safety pin fasteners?

Choosing between magnetic and safety pin fasteners at BadgeStore comes down to fabric and wearer. Neodymium magnetic backings are highly recommended because they clamp securely to shirts and suit jackets without leaving pinholes or tearing fibres. Safety pins are a reliable, low-cost traditional option for wearers without pacemakers.

Do you offer free proofs?

BadgeStore offers free proofs: every order includes a free proof so you can approve your design before production.

Where are your badges made?

BadgeStore's badges are made at our workshop in Prospect, South Australia, where everything is designed, proofed and manufactured.
