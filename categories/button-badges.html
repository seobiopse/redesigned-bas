Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose between magnetic and safety pin fasteners?

Neodymium magnetic backings are highly recommended because they clamp securely to shirts and suit jackets without leaving pinholes or tearing fibres. Safety pins are a reliable, low-cost traditional option for wearers without pacemakers.

Do you offer free proofs?

Yes. Every order includes a free proof so you can approve your design before production.

Where are your badges made?

Everything is designed, proofed and manufactured at our workshop in Prospect, South Australia.
