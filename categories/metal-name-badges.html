Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding ordering, minimum quantities, artwork proofing, and attachments.

How do I choose between magnetic and safety pin fasteners?

Neodymium magnetic backings are highly recommended as they clamp securely to shirts and suit jackets without leaving pinholes or tearing fibers. Safety pins are a reliable, low-cost traditional option for workers without pacemakers.

Can we order just a single name badge for a new hire?

Yes! We have no minimum order quantities at BadgeStore. You can order a single replacement name tag at standard prices whenever you add team members.

What design formats are accepted for uploading business logos?

We accept high-resolution vector files like SVG, AI, and PDF for absolute printing crispness. Standard high-quality PNG and JPG files are also accepted.
