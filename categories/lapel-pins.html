Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding ordering, minimum quantities, artwork proofing, and attachments.

What lapel do you put a pin on?

The lapel you put a pin on is the left lapel: a lapel pin from BadgeStore is traditionally worn on the left lapel of a jacket or blazer, just below the collar notch.

Can enamel lapel pins go through airport security (TSA)?

Enamel lapel pins from BadgeStore can go through airport security (TSA): enamel lapel pins are permitted through TSA and airport security screening in both carry-on and checked luggage, since they contain no restricted materials.

Where can I find custom metal lapel pins in Australia?

Custom metal lapel pins are made by specialist badge and pin manufacturers who die-strike or enamel-fill a design to order — call BadgeStore on 1300 862 637 to check current availability for metal lapel pins.

Does BadgeStore manufacture custom lapel pins?

BadgeStore doesn't currently list lapel pins as a stocked product line — call 1300 862 637 to discuss a custom lapel pin run and we'll scope availability and lead time for your design.

How are custom lapel pins usually made?

Custom lapel pins from BadgeStore are usually made by die-striking in metal and finishing with enamel fill or soft enamel colour, or printed with an offset/epoxy dome finish for more complex full-colour designs.

What's the difference between a lapel pin and a name badge?

The difference between a lapel pin and a name badge at BadgeStore is size and purpose: a lapel pin is a small, decorative item usually pinned through a jacket lapel for events or recognition, while a name badge is a larger, wearable item designed to display a name and logo for everyday staff identification.
