Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding ordering, minimum quantities, artwork proofing, and attachments.

What is the difference between soft enamel and hard enamel custom pins?

Soft enamel pins are baked after paint fills, leaving recessed colored wells below the raised metal lines for a textured feel. Hard enamel pins are overfilled and polished completely flat with the metal, creating a glass-like finish.

What clasp types are available?

We offer traditional metal butterfly clutches, rubber clutches (ideal for wearing next to the skin), and magnetic pinless backings for garment safety.

What is the standard turnaround time for custom lapel pins?

Because lapel pins require unique mold strikes and detailed hand-painting, production takes 10-15 business days from final proof approval.
