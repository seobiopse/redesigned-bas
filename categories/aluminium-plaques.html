Aluminium Plaques Buyer's Guide & Specifications

Everything your procurement, HR, or event team needs to know regarding materials, fixings, and delivery times across Australia.

1

Materials & Craftsmanship

At BadgeStore Australia, our aluminium plaques come in 14 sizes, from 50mm x 25mm to 450mm x 250mm, and six colours with white text. There is no minimum order.

  • Material: premium anodised aluminium, 1mm thick, with a durable 16+ micron anodic coating.
  • Colours: Satin Black, Gloss Red, Gloss Blue, Gloss Green, Gloss Gold and Matt Bronze, all with white text.
  • Engraving: laser engraving turns the anodising white, giving a highly visible mark, especially on dark colours.
  • Serialising: plaques can also be stamped with metal number punches.
